# Go to market

> Updated for D-028 to D-031. The diaspora pilot below is now the Phase 3 "remote lines" segment.

## First users (D-030)

Lagos Android users with a data plan and a prepaid meter at home: young professionals, students, small business owners. They run out of data mid-call and lose light without warning, every week.

## Promise

"Never run out mid-call. Never come home to a dead meter."

## Price (D-031)

Data and airtime at the same price as buying them directly; Constant earns the partner discount. ₦100 per electricity token delivered. A monthly Plus plan later.

## How they pay

A bank transfer into their own Constant account number, from any bank app.

## Where we find them

1. The founder's own circle, then campus and workplace WhatsApp groups: the Phase 0 testers.
2. The moment of pain: "Your data ran out mid-call? Constant tops up before it happens." Short videos on X, TikTok and Instagram showing the warning and the automatic top-up.
3. Referral: invite a friend, both get free data on the first top-up.
4. Family: "Add Mum's line" brings in the next household, and the diaspora segment after it.

## Measures

- Data: share of active users who ran out while autopilot was on (target: under 1 in 200 sessions); weekly active users; wallet top-ups per user per month.
- Light: surprise outages before vs after; readings sent when asked; spare tokens keyed within an hour.

# Go to market (pilot)

Decisions D-020 to D-027 in one page.

## Who

Nigerians abroad (UK, US, Canada, Ireland, Germany) whose parents or relatives live in a Lagos house on IKEDC prepaid. The owner pays; the site phone is the parent.

## Promise

"Your family's light is paid on the days you choose. They get the token by SMS. You get a receipt. It never spends more than you set."

## Price

₦100 per token delivered, taken from the meter's balance. No subscription. Alert mode is free.

## How they pay

A bank transfer, from any Nigerian bank app or any remittance app that pays out to a Nigerian account, into the meter's own account number. No card, no app.

## Where we find them

1. The founder's network, then their families' networks: the first 10 pilot owners (PLAN B1).
2. Diaspora WhatsApp groups: hometown unions, alumni associations, churches. The founder posts in person, not an ad.
3. Every token SMS says who it is from. Parents tell siblings, and siblings abroad are the next owners.
4. After the pilot: a referral link (wa.me with a code). Both owners get one free fee.

## Pilot (4 weeks, 10 owners)

- Run by hand before the code is ready: the founder buys on the owner's days from a company account and sends the token and the receipt text. Charge the ₦100 from day one, because a free pilot does not test the price.
- Measure: funded a second time (target ≥ 6 of 10), tokens delivered on the chosen morning (target 100%), LOWs per site per week, support messages per owner, and cost per vend.
- Exit: if at least 6 of 10 fund again and the cost per vend is under ₦100, build steps 2–7 for real and move them onto the software.

## Next segment

Lagos landlords and employers with a caretaker. Same product, more sites per owner. Revisit pricing (D-020) for them.
