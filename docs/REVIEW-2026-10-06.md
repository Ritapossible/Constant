## Things the review missed, added here

- **Non-custodial dollar pots are a regulatory advantage.** With spend permissions, the user's money stays in their own smart account; Constant only holds a capped right to charge. That's lighter than holding naira people can withdraw.
- **Crypto needs its own legal opinion** (Investments and Securities Act 2025, SEC Nigeria rules for digital-asset service providers) before the dollar path goes live (PLAN B12).
- **The embedded-wallet mobile SDK** may decide the app's stack (PLAN B11).
- **Where Nigerians hold USDT today** (mostly on exchanges and other networks): the deposit screen must coach "choose Base" or deposits get lost.
